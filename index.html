FAQ

Honest answers before you start.

Everything about quitting weed, withdrawal, and how CannaClear supports both cutting down and stopping completely.

What is CannaClear and who is it for?
CannaClear is an iOS app for people who want to quit weed, reduce cannabis use, or get more structure during a cannabis detox. It combines a sobriety tracker, craving tools, and progress check-ins so recovery feels visible instead of chaotic — designed for everyday support, not pressure or shame.
What does weed withdrawal usually feel like?
Common symptoms include poor sleep, irritability, anxiety, cravings, lower appetite, and mood swings. Most people feel the strongest symptoms around days 3 to 7. It can feel intense, but it usually improves steadily with time and consistency — and knowing that in advance makes it far easier to get through.
How long do cannabis cravings last?
Cravings come in waves rather than lasting all day. A single urge typically rises, peaks, and fades within about 15–20 minutes — even if it returns later. Over time they become less frequent and easier to manage. The SOS tools are built exactly for those minutes.
Does it work if I only want to cut down, not quit?
Yes. Some people use CannaClear to stop completely, others to reduce first and rebuild control. The app supports both paths with tracking, structure, and calm daily guidance — a short reset, a 30-day break, or a long-term change.
Can a tracker really make a difference?
Tracking turns vague effort into something you can see. Sober days, cravings, and patterns become visible progress — especially during the hard first weeks, when memory alone tells you nothing is changing. Seeing the numbers move is often what keeps people going.
Is my data private?
Yes. Journal entries and check-ins are not linked to your identity. There's no social feed and nothing to share — this is a private space for your reset. See the privacy policy for details.
Is CannaClear a medical service?
No. CannaClear supports people who want to reduce or quit cannabis, but it doesn't replace professional medical, psychological, or emergency support. If you're struggling severely, please reach out to a professional.
