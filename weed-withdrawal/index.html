Weed Withdrawal: What Actually Happens

A practical weed withdrawal guide to help you stop smoking weed and stay consistent through each detox phase.

If you have tried quitting weed, you have probably felt it:

Anxiety.

Restlessness.

Insomnia.

Racing thoughts.

That is withdrawal, and it is completely normal.

Common Weed Withdrawal Symptoms

  • Anxiety
  • Irritability
  • Sleep problems
  • Brain fog
  • Loss of appetite

These symptoms are temporary.

Weed Withdrawal Timeline

Day 1-3: Cravings and restlessness

Day 4-7: Anxiety peaks

Day 7-14: Sleep improves slowly

Day 14+: Mental clarity returns

How to Reduce Withdrawal Symptoms

  • Stay consistent
  • Track your progress
  • Avoid triggers
  • Understand what is happening

The more clarity you have, the easier it gets.
