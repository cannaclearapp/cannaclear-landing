Before You Quit: Make It Real

  • Set a specific quit date in the next 7 to 14 days
  • Tell one trusted person for accountability
  • Identify your top 3 triggers before day one
  • Prepare one SOS action for cravings (breathing, walk, or call)

Structure beats motivation. A clear plan makes cannabis detox more stable from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to quit weed?

Acute withdrawal usually resolves in 2 to 3 weeks. Lasting habit change often takes 60 to 90 days.

Can I quit on my own?

Yes. Many people do, especially when they track progress, plan cravings, and stay accountable.

What if I relapse once?

Treat it as data, not failure. Reset quickly, identify the trigger, and continue.
