Medical Disclaimer

CannaClear provides educational information about cannabis withdrawal, recovery, cravings, behavior change, and long-term support. This content is intended to inform and support readers, not to replace individualized medical care.

Educational Purpose

The information on CannaClear is published for educational purposes only. It is designed to help people better understand recovery, but it should not be treated as a substitute for professional evaluation or treatment.

Articles describe general patterns found in research and public health guidance. They cannot account for your medical history, mental health, medications, other substance use, pattern of cannabis use, or individual circumstances.

No Medical Advice

CannaClear does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Reading an article on this site does not create a clinician-patient relationship.

Do not start, stop, or change prescribed medication or treatment based only on information from CannaClear. Questions about symptoms, medication interactions, pregnancy, underlying conditions, or a treatment plan should be discussed with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

The CannaClear app is a self-tracking and educational support tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, crisis service, or substitute for professional care.

Emergency Information

If you experience suicidal thoughts, a risk of harming yourself or someone else, chest pain, trouble breathing, loss of consciousness, severe confusion, or any medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately or go to the nearest emergency department.

CannaClear is available internationally, and emergency numbers and crisis services differ by location. Use the official emergency or crisis service for the country where you are physically located. Do not wait for an email response from CannaClear during an urgent situation.

When to Consult a Healthcare Professional

Contact a healthcare professional if symptoms are severe, persistent, worsening, or interfering significantly with sleep, mood, safety, or daily functioning. Professional support can be especially important if you have a history of depression, panic, trauma, or other mental health concerns.

You should also seek professional guidance if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, use other substances, take prescription medication, have a heart or neurological condition, have previously experienced psychosis or mania, or are unsure whether a symptom is related to cannabis withdrawal.

Timelines and Individual Results

Withdrawal, recovery, and drug-detection timelines on CannaClear are estimates based on available evidence and commonly observed patterns. They are not guarantees. Frequency of use, THC potency, duration of use, metabolism, sleep, health, and other factors can change an individual experience.

Feeling better does not prove that all withdrawal effects have ended, and feeling worse than an average timeline does not by itself identify a medical condition. A qualified professional can evaluate symptoms in their full context.
